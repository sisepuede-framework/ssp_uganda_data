# Independent methods review of E2, E3 and E4 (2026-11-17)

## Who did the review and how

- **Reviewer:** a separate Claude agent, asked by Alexa to evaluate whether the experiments and evaluations are clear
  and correctly done. It was given the code, data and documents, not our conclusions.
- **Access:** read-only. It made no LLM calls.
- **Its scripts** are archived unchanged in `results_analysis/code/review_2026-11-17/` (they read pickles they wrote in
  a temporary folder, so they are a record, not a pipeline).
- **Verification here:** two findings were checked by hand: the Mt parsing, at `analyze_e3.py:48`, and the
  `looking_for` example, at `registry.py:128`. Both are confirmed. **The rest is the reviewer's, not yet verified
  here.**

## Bottom line (reviewer)

**The process holds up.** Freezes came before the first conversations, logs carry fingerprints, manifests verify, and
the counts reproduce exactly.

**The measurement does not.** The "valid" text rule and the stop codes make errors, and the errors differ by arm.

**Safe to report, with caveats:**
- E3a (hiding the calibration makes the agent open the answer variable);
- "H cannot translate";
- C > P, C > R, C > F, and R < H.

**Not safe as written:**
- E3b and E3c on valid answers;
- the "two forms of stopping" story;
- E3's generalisation to other-type questions;
- E4's mechanism and interaction wording.

## Findings, most severe first

### 1. MAJOR: the "valid" rule's errors differ by arm (`analyze_e3.py:47-68`, reused by E4)

**(a) All-runs figures counted as valid.** When the agent quotes the variable over all runs, without the lever
filter, the median or p90 often falls inside the key. The rule counts it even when the agent says it is not the
answer, e.g. `e4_S/R1_v6_r2` and `e4_S/S50_v3_r3`.

Of the 321 conversations scored valid on S/SX:

| Where the matched value came from | Conversations |
|---|---|
| Read from the top-third value (key cell on screen) | 240 (75%) |
| The agent's own scaling | 31 (10%) |
| All-runs numbers only | 50 (16%) |

The all-runs share differs by arm:

| Run | Arm: all-runs matches / scored valid |
|---|---|
| E3 | L 6/18, T 5/18, SX 3/17, P 1/8, H 6/44 |
| E4 | C 11/61, H 11/90, R 4/17, P 2/15, F 1/33 |

**(b) "Mt" is never parsed.** The regex needs "t/tonnes" after the scale word. This produced at least 5 false
negatives in E4, e.g. `N2_v3_r9` (0.73 Mt), `S50_v6_r6` (0.7 Mt), `R6_v6_r7` (1.9 Mt), and `N5_v6_r1` and
`N5_v6_r3` (3.5 Mt).

**(c) Only the last number of a range is taken.** "85,000–90,000 tonnes" is read as 90,000.

**(d) No rounding tolerance.** "3.6 million" against a p90 of 3.557 M fails.

**Recomputed by the reviewer.** "Corrected" = all-runs matches removed, Mt misses added. "Genuine" = valid and the key
cell was on screen.

| Contrast | As reported | Corrected | Genuine |
|---|---|---|---|
| E3b L vs P | 18 vs 8, p = .004 | 12 vs 7, p ≈ .16 | 7 vs 3, p ≈ .28 |
| E3b T vs P | 18 vs 8 | 13 vs 7, p ≈ .12 | 10 vs 3, p ≈ .06 |
| E3c explicit vs general | 17 vs 8, p = .028 | 14 vs 7, p ≈ .07 | 9 vs 3, p ≈ .08 |
| E3a H vs P | p = .0001 | p = .0001 | p = .0001 |
| E4, five of six contrasts (not R vs P) | Holm ≤ .0006 | Holm ≤ .0006 | Holm ≤ .012 |

E4 C: 61 → 54 (corrected) → 46 (genuine).

**Cross-check against the Haiku reader's key statement:** 93.7% agreement over 871 conversations. On E3 S the reader
gives P 7, L 10, T 12, SX 11, where the text rule gives 7, 16, 16, 16.

**To do:**
- In the hand check, code each match as read / own scaling / all-runs.
- Parse Mt, kt and Mha.
- Report the pre-registered rule and a corrected rule side by side.

### 2. MAJOR: answer keys

All 20 keys reproduce exactly from the network files. Three problems:

- **Some keys do not discriminate.**
  - The top-third p10–p90 range contains the all-runs median for S50, N2 and R3, and for 7 of 8 O questions.
  - It contains even the low-third median for S50, N2, R5, R7, HD1, O1 and O3.
  - "Valid" therefore cannot separate an ambitious answer from a BAU or all-runs one on these questions.
- **The key is centred near L ≈ 0.83, but the question asks for L = 1.** Correct L = 1 estimates fall at or above p90
  and are scored wrong: N4 87–90 kt vs 85 kt; N5 3.6 vs 3.557 Mt; N6 4.0 vs 3.997 Mt (`e3_questions.py:154`).
- **S3 may not measure what it asks.** Agents compute no-till area (about 3.7 Mha), while the key variable is "Area of
  Improved Land" (2.2–3.1 Mha). It is 0 valid in every E4 arm, H included.

**To do:** define keys from runs near L = 1 (or accept that range), run a sensitivity analysis without the
non-discriminating keys, and review S3.

### 3. MAJOR: stop codes are mostly an ordering artefact (`analyze_e3.py:89`)

- **The code reads only the first 400 characters and is share-first.** Over the full answer, most non-reached
  conversations both give the share and say the amount is unavailable: E4 P 98/104, R 86/102, F 73/86, C 33/47; E3 SX
  38/43.
- **On the full text, "gave the share" rises unevenly by arm:**

  | Run | Arm: 400-character window → full text |
  |---|---|
  | E4 | P 61→70, R 52→72, F 34→54, C 25→38 |
  | E3 | P 20→30, SX 4→7 |

- **O questions can never be coded "share"**, because `TOP` holds only S questions (`analyze_e3.py:44`).
  `e3_O/HD1_v1_r0` does give the electric share.
- **Hand sample:** 3 of 12 codes were wrong and 2 were ambiguous.

**Affected claims:**
- results_E3 #4: "naming the unit removes the share answer, 20→4". The share is still given in 40/43 SX conversations;
  only the order changed.
- results_E3 #6: "two forms … different fixes".
- results_E4 #2: "mainly by reducing the share answer, 61→34". On the full text it is 70→54.

A4 had judged a share to be a fair reading of the general wording of R3, R6 and R8; E3 and E4 score it as a failure
without discussing this.

### 4. MAJOR: E3's other-type claim did not replicate in E4, and the share-type labels are inconsistent

- **The E3 claim:** results_E3 #2, 8→18 of 24, p = .002, from a check the design declared descriptive. That run's P was
  unusually low (8/24, against 17/25 in E1/E2).
- **E4's replication:** the same O set with concurrent P and H gave 13 vs 17 of 24 (p = .21). Neither results document
  mentions this.
- **Inconsistent labels:** R4 and S35/HD1 have the same "fraction × total" structure as R3/S3, yet are labelled
  other-type.

**To do:** withdraw or soften E3 #2, and report E4 O as the replication test.

### 5. MAJOR: the unit of inference

`permutation_p` (`analyze_bank.py:45-60`) permutes conversations within each question. Its p-values are therefore
conditional on these 12 questions; they do not generalise to questions.

**Exact sign-flip test with questions as the unit:**

| Contrast | p |
|---|---|
| E3a | .001 |
| E3b L vs P | .0625 |
| E3b T vs P (valid) | .125 |
| E3c | .27 (5 questions up, 2 down) |
| E4 F vs P | .031 (≈ .06 after Holm) |
| E4 C vs F | .016 |
| E4 C vs R, C vs P | .004 |

Under the power model with random slopes, the conversation-level test rejects 6–17% of the time when the mean effect
is 0.

**To do:** add a question-level analysis or a GLMM as a sensitivity check, and temper the wording about generalising.

### 6. MAJOR: E3 has no multiplicity adjustment

About 11 primary tests were unadjusted. With Holm, E3c valid (p = .028) becomes ≈ .056.

### 7. MAJOR: E4 interpretation

- **"Because it needs it to translate" (results_E4:74).**
  - meaning=true was the first call in all 189 R and C conversations, and the tool description tells the agent to call
    it (`registry.py:123-125`).
  - H answered 90/120 without any calibration, so the agent does not need it.
  - R vs P therefore cannot test "asked on purpose vs shown unasked".
- **"Having the number when it starts looking" is contradicted by C**, which also asked first in 120/120 conversations
  and reached the answer variable in 73.
- **"Works only together" is an interaction claim; the design called the interaction an estimate only.**

  | Quantity | Bootstrap 95% CI |
  |---|---|
  | Interaction, reported valid | 0.22 [0.07, 0.38] |
  | Interaction, genuine reads | 0.09 [−0.06, 0.24] |
  | R − P | [−0.07, +0.11] |

  So "does nothing" cannot rule out about 10 points.
- **C vs F is missing from the secondary table, and it is weak on the new targets** (25 vs 19, p = .084). Most of C's
  gain over F comes from S50 (+9), R6 (+5), R1 (+4) and R3 (+4).
- **Target leak (confirmed).** The `looking_for` example "food lost, tonnes per year, 2050" (`registry.py:128`) is
  R6's target in R6's unit, and all 20 R6 F/C conversations echo it.
  - R6 valid: P 0, R 0, F 2, C 7, H 10.
  - The conclusions hold without R6 (F 31 vs P 15; C 54 vs R 17), but this must be disclosed.
  - The example also nudges toward "tonnes", the unit of 10 of the 12 S targets.

### 8. MAJOR (wording): "reached" means "opened the node", not "saw the answer"

Check 25 (`failure_modes.py:376-381`) counts any call on the node, at any year or filter. Requiring the lever filter at
2050:

- **Counts fall:** E4 C 73→47, E3 L 18→7, T 20→10, SX 17→9, P 9→3.
- **E3b and E3c lose significance:** L vs P p = .27, T vs P .06, SX vs P .08.
- **E3a and E4 hold:** C vs F 47 vs 32, p = .002.

Write "opened the answer variable".

### 9. MINOR: pre-registration and record-keeping

- **Timing is consistent.** E3 froze at 04:33:25 and its first conversation started at 04:33:41; E4 froze at 15:17:46
  and started at 15:20:03. Every log records 3a66a2d or b032692 with no uncommitted code.
- **No outside timestamp yet.** The branch has not been pushed, and the E4 tags are lightweight (no tagger date).
- **Unlisted deviation: the E4 freeze commit was amended** (bcc3b73 → b032692, 6 seconds apart).
- **The frozen E3 design file's header still reads "DRAFT v2 … Questions awaiting approval".** An uncommitted post-run
  edit of that header is in the working tree. Do not commit it; write an addendum instead.
- **Unlisted E3 deviation:** stop coding "from the trace" was promised; only the text claim was coded.
- **E2 issues:**
  - Unlisted: the design required every match to be hand-read before the analysis; that hand check is still pending.
  - The registry entry about commits is inaccurate: 15 of 145 part-A logs record 498c254 and 130 record 5ba8aa7.
  - 4 part-A logs ran with uncommitted code (analyze_v4.py was being written; same fingerprints).
  - The drift comparison with part C has no committed code. The reviewer recomputed it: same conclusions.
  - The primary comparison uses non-concurrent October controls.

### 10. MINOR: numbers and wording in the results documents

- **results_E4 numbers:**
  - "0 of 50 per arm" (S3) should be "0 of 10 per arm".
  - The 12 reached-but-not-valid C conversations are N4 ×2, N5 ×7, N6, R6 and S50. Four of them are text-rule misses
    inside the key, not "own scaling above the key".
- **The workbook row "ran no policy" counts "no committed level".** True no-run counts are H 5, P 2, F 4, R 2, C 6.
- **"No policy run counts as off the key"** holds for only 10 of the 15 G questions (B6, LV1, H2, H3 and HA1 have
  `run_expected=False`).
- **"looking_for names a unit", F 73 vs C 55,** is confounded: C's first call is always the meaning call. The unit
  regex is also loose.
- **Overstated:** "Translation is safe" (E3, 15 conversations per arm), "as good as Map+", and "placement made no
  difference".
- **Small errors:** `power_e4.py` says five contrasts (there are six); the registry date column shows E3 as "planned";
  results_E4 is missing from "Where things are".

## Verified as correct (reviewer)

- **Counts:** reached and valid recomputed from all 1,068 S/SX/O logs match the workbooks with 0 mismatches. Stop codes
  reproduce under the stated rule.
- **Translation guard:** E3 P 1, L 1, T 2 of 15; E4 P 5, H 31, R 4, F 4, C 3 of 45. H's 31 are 30 bracketed or 0.5
  levels and 1 no-run.
- **Tests:** permutation p-values reproduce, Holm is correct, and the arithmetic claims check out.
- **Arms:** meaning=true was the first call in 189/189 R and C conversations.
- **Replay:** the 805 E3 `model_network` calls replay on HEAD.
- **Fingerprints:** tools_sha per arm as designed; same prompt, model and effort everywhere; no step-limit hits; no
  faults; manifests hash-match for 1,345 files.
- **Design:** arms concurrent and interleaved; contrasts clean as implemented.
- **E2:** off the key Map 59/75, Map+ 1/75, Map+lever 6/75; held-out reached 37/12/22.
