Model selection
Why MultiOutputRegressor won

The comparison

Variant A (deployed) — MultiOutputRegressor(XGBRegressor): one independent gradient-boosted tree per output target.

Variant B (not selected) — XGBRegressor(multi_strategy='multi_output_tree'): a single shared tree across all targets (XGBoost 2.0+ native).

Variant B looked appealing — one model, shared structure, faster inference. But it collapsed on cost and co-benefit targets: R² drops from 0.97 → 0.39 on near-term costs, and from 0.99 → 0.46 on co-benefits. These are the metrics most relevant to policy decisions.

Variant A dominates on 57 of 59 targets, and especially on all 48 sector×year breakdowns needed for the stacked chart.
