Its attributes and the values of each of its fields appear here: the median and p10–p90 over the runs, with the lever low (0–⅓), middle (⅓–⅔) or high (⅔–1), every other lever and uncertainty varying.

How to read it. A line means SISEPUEDE uses the left variable to compute the right one (traced from one run of the model code); it says nothing about direction or size. NemoMod, the electricity optimisation, is one box: what goes in and what comes out are known, not the steps inside. Which input feeds which variable is only as fine as the code: some in-between outputs sit beside the chain, not on it. Box values: the sum of the medians of the variable's fields, lever low → high (for fractions and unitless variables, the first field). Plan, rules and test: docs/rdm_network_plan.md.
